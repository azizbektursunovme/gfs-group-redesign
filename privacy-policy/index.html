Privacy Policy

Last updated April 24, 2026

GFS GROUP LLC ("GFS," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through our website and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out a form on our website, apply for a driving opportunity, request information, or otherwise contact us. This information may include:

  • first name and last name
  • phone number
  • email address
  • location or address information you choose to provide
  • driving qualification details you choose to provide
  • message content and inquiry details

We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and basic website usage data.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • respond to your inquiries
  • review driver applications
  • contact you about driving opportunities
  • schedule interviews, follow-up calls, or onboarding-related communication
  • provide recruiting updates, document reminders, and support replies
  • improve our website, services, and recruiting process
  • send text messages or emails based on your consent preferences
  • comply with legal obligations

Cookies, Tracking Technologies, and Analytics

Our website may use cookies, pixels, tags, scripts, and similar tracking technologies to improve website functionality, understand user interactions, analyze traffic, remember preferences, and support marketing and advertising activities.

These technologies may collect information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, and general website usage activity.

We may use analytics and tracking tools provided by third-party service providers to help us measure website performance, improve user experience, evaluate the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns, and better understand how visitors interact with our website.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the website.

SMS Communications and Consent

If you provide your phone number through our website forms and explicitly consent by selecting one or both optional SMS consent checkboxes, GFS GROUP LLC may send you text messages based on your selected preferences.

If you select the non-marketing SMS checkbox, you may receive non-marketing text messages related to your driver application, recruiting updates, interview scheduling, qualification follow-ups, document reminders, onboarding reminders, support replies, and other job opportunity-related notifications.

If you select the marketing SMS checkbox, you may receive marketing text messages related to company news, announcements, recruiting campaigns, promotional updates, and special offers.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for assistance.

No Sharing of SMS Consent Data

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How We Share Information

We may share your information only as reasonably necessary to operate our business and provide our services, including with:

  • service providers and contractors who help us operate our website, systems, and communications
  • recruiting, CRM, analytics, automation, hosting, and support providers
  • legal or regulatory authorities if required by law

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to help protect the personal information we collect from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to

personal information is limited to authorized personnel and service providers who need it to perform their duties. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to:

  • request access to the personal information you provided
  • request correction of inaccurate information
  • request that we stop contacting you for marketing purposes
  • request deletion of your information where applicable

You may opt out of SMS communications by replying STOP. You may opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link, where available.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or tools, including external application platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.
